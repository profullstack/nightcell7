[Intro - distant horn, wind over a mountain, choir swelling slowly, instrumental]

[Verse 1 - male baritone, half-sung half-rapped, steady and noble]
On the rainbow bridge there's a watchman with a horn,
Hears the grass grow in the valley, hears the wool upon the shorn.
He was set there by the treaty when the fighting had an end,
Not to guard a single kingdom, but to see and to defend.
He is small beside the armies, and he carries no great blade,
Just the eyes that count the ships and just the book in which they're weighed.
If the watchman sees it clearly, then the lie cannot be told,
So the merchant sent the ravens with a sack of silver gold.

So we stand beside the watchman with our shields turned to the dark,
