# Þrøngva: When the Ravens Lied

Second album. Same band, same sound as _After the Winter of Want_: Nordic
Viking metal fused with hip-hop. A male baritone half-raps the verses, a female
contralto carries the pre-choruses and bridges, and a mixed choir with gang
shouts lands the hooks over drop-D riffs and war drums.

**Concept.** Odin sends two ravens out each dawn, Huginn (thought) and Muninn
(memory), and believes what they bring back. On this album someone has taught
the ravens to lie. Forged voices and forged smoke tell each hall the other
struck first, and a merchant sells spears to both. It is the game's MIRAGE told
as saga: a war manufactured so someone can profit from it. The first album
starved the wolf called Want. This one refuses a Ragnarök ordered from a
catalogue, and ends on a true dawn to answer FALSE DAWN.

Content rule (docs/content-and-culture.md): no real nation is named, and
neither side is the enemy. The villain is the lie and the one who profits.

## Format

This file is the source of truth for `tools/music/synth_album.py`, which
synthesises the album from it.

- `# NN. Title` starts a track.
- `style:` and `avoid:` add to the album-wide styles for that track.
- `## Section | local style | seconds` starts a section. Lines under it are
  sung or rapped; a section with no lines is instrumental.
- `ALBUM-STYLE:` and `ALBUM-AVOID:` apply to every track.
- Everything before the first track is notes and is ignored.

ALBUM-STYLE: Nordic Viking metal fused with hip-hop, drop-D down-tuned heavy guitars, pounding war drums, tagelharpa and bone flute, deep throat-singing drones, cinematic strings, male baritone half-rap lead, female contralto lead, massive mixed choir, gang shouts, anthemic, epic, modern heavy production, clear intelligible English vocals
ALBUM-AVOID: autotune, EDM drop, lo-fi, comedy, spoken-word only, children's choir, country, reggae
